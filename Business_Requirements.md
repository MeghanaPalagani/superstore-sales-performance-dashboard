# Business Requirements Document

## Project Title

Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis Dashboard

---

## Background

The organization wants to monitor sales performance, profitability, customer activity, and regional performance using a centralized dashboard.

The dashboard should provide management with a clear understanding of overall business performance and help identify opportunities for improving profitability.

---

## Business Problem

Business stakeholders currently lack a consolidated view of:

* Sales performance
* Profitability trends
* Customer activity
* Regional performance
* Category-level performance
* Discount impact on profits

A reporting solution is required to support data-driven decision making.

---

## Project Goals

The dashboard should:

* Monitor overall business performance.
* Track sales and profit trends over time.
* Identify top-performing categories and regions.
* Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability.
* Provide detailed drill-down analysis for business users.

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Sales Metrics

* Total Sales
* Sales Trend

### Profitability Metrics

* Total Profit
* Profit Margin %

### Operational Metrics

* Total Orders
* Total Customers

---

## Business Questions

1. How much did we sell?
2. How much profit did we make?
3. How many orders were placed?
4. How many customers purchased?
5. How are sales changing over time?
6. Which categories generate the most sales?
7. Which regions generate the most sales?
8. Are discounts affecting profitability?

---

## Dashboard Requirements

### Overview Page

Required Visuals:

* KPI Cards
* Sales Trend Over Time
* Sales by Category
* Sales by Region
* Profit by Category
* Profit Margin by Sub-Category

---

### Profitability & Discount Analysis Page

Required Visuals:

* Profit by Sub-Category
* Sales by Sub-Category
* Average Discount by Sub-Category
* Discount vs Profit Scatter Plot

---

### Detailed Analysis Page

Required Visuals:

* Sales Distribution by Category
* Detailed Performance Table

Required Filters:

* Category
* Region
* Order Year

--- 

## Success Criteria

The dashboard will be considered successful if users can:

* Monitor business performance quickly.
* Identify profitable and unprofitable segments.
* Understand the impact of discounts.
* Analyze performance by category, sub-category, and region.
* Access detailed transactional summaries through interactive filtering.
